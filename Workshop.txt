description=[h2]Crafting Process[/h2]
description=Creating a light bulb is a multi-step process that involves both glassblowing and electrical work.
description=
description=[b]1. Make a Blown Glass Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Pliers (may degrade)
description=[*] 1 Glass Blowing Pipe
description=[*] 1 Small Ceramic Crucible with Molten Glass
description=[/list]
description=Skill: [b]Glassmaking 2[/b]
description=
description=[b]2. Make the Base of Light Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Wire
description=[*] 1 Electric Wire
description=[*] 1 Scrap Metal
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[/list]
description=Skill: [b]Electricity 3[/b]
description=
description=[b]3. Build the Vacuum Pump[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Tire Pump
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[*] 1 Duct Tape
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]4. Assemble the Light Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Blown Glass Bulb
description=[*] 1 Light Bulb Base
description=[*] 1 Glue
description=[*] 1 Vacuum Pump (kept)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]5. (Optional) Revert Vacuum Pump into Tire Pump[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Vacuum Pump
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[*] 1 Duct Tape
description=[/list]
