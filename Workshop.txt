description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=[i]Even when the world feels out of breath and shrouded in darkness, there will always be someone blowing glass and tinkering with wires. These bulbs, forged through patience and resourcefulness, remain as fragile as the thought of a morning during a long night... but they shine like a smile in the midst of shadows.
description=After all, if your survival doesn’t bring you a little light, you might as well wander aimlessly in the dark.[/i]
description=
description=This mod allows you to craft light bulbs using glassblowing and electrical skills.
description=
description=[h2]New Items[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Blown Glass Bulb[/b] – A fragile component formed through glassblowing
description=[*] [b]Light Bulb Base[/b] – Metal base to connect the filament and circuit
description=[*] [b]Vacuum Pump[/b] – Required to remove air during assembly
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Crafting Process[/h2]
description=Creating a light bulb is a multi-step process that involves both glassblowing and electrical work.
description=
description=[b]1. Make a Blown Glass Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Pliers (may degrade)
description=[*] 1 Glass Blowing Pipe
description=[*] 1 Small Ceramic Crucible with Molten Glass
description=[/list]
description=Skill: [b]Glassmaking 2[/b]
description=
description=[b]2. Make the Base of Light Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Wire
description=[*] 1 Electric Wire
description=[*] 1 Scrap Metal
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[/list]
description=Skill: [b]Electricity 3[/b]
description=
description=[b]3. Build the Vacuum Pump[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Tire Pump
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[*] 1 Duct Tape
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]4. Assemble the Light Bulb[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Blown Glass Bulb
description=[*] 1 Light Bulb Base
description=[*] 1 Glue
description=[*] 1 Vacuum Pump (kept)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]5. (Optional) Revert Vacuum Pump into Tire Pump[/b]
description=Requires:[list]
description=[*] 1 Vacuum Pump
description=[*] 1 Screwdriver (kept)
description=[*] 1 Duct Tape
description=[/list]
description=
